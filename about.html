Wheel of blade

Spin the wheel, land a grind.

A trick randomiser for aggressive inline skating — 49 tricks, 21 variations, zero excuses.

How to use

  • Select trick categories with the checkboxes
  • Tap Spin — the wheel picks a random trick
  • Tap the result to mark it landed, missed, or skipped
  • Skip removes a trick for the session; Reshuffle picks a fresh 12
  • Toggle Add Variation to append a random modifier to each result
